About

Hello, I'm Heather.

I did not find this work. This work found me.

There's a moment many people reach when the life they've been living stops being livable. The coping stops working, and the pain underneath gets louder than everything they've used to quiet it.

I know that moment. I lived it.

The descent

I grew up with childhood trauma I didn't choose and didn't understand, and it shaped how I saw myself, who I loved, and what I believed I deserved. In my twenties I lost my uterus, and with it the future I had quietly assumed I would have. I found my way into a relationship that became abusive and trauma-bonded, and I carried the marks of that for a long time afterward: CPTSD, dissociation, addiction, love addiction, and betrayal bonding.

Finding my way out was not a single decision. It was years of sobriety and recovery, held by AA, SLAA, CODA, and Al-Anon, alongside extensive therapeutic, somatic, shadow, and spiritual work. That is how I began to come home to myself. I share this with discretion, not detail, because it is the doorway into this work, not the whole of it.

The spiritual and ceremonial path

For eighteen years I have walked a dedicated ceremonial and spiritual path, including within the Santo Daime tradition. That path has held mystical illumination alongside shadow work, and ancestral and spiritual dimensions of healing I could not have reached any other way. This is my lived understanding, not a claim on universal truth, and I hold it that way: essential to my own liberation, and offered without asking anyone else to share my worldview. Whatever your faith, spiritual orientation, or lack of one, you are welcome here exactly as you are.

Professional formation

Alongside that personal path, I built a professional one. I have been a licensed therapeutic massage therapist for fifteen years, and for the past five years I have worked as a psychedelic guide and integration specialist, supporting people through preparation, medicine experiences, and integration. That work has brought me alongside people navigating a wide range of concerns, far beyond my own history: severe anxiety and depression, complex trauma and abuse, substance and process addictions, grief, dissociation, spiritual emergence, and life transitions, among others.

I am currently pursuing an MA in Psychedelic Therapy, in progress, through AWE Ecstatic Mysticism and Ubiquity University, and I am becoming an integrative psychedelic therapist. I am not yet licensed to call myself a therapist, and I hold that distinction carefully, alongside ongoing training in trauma-informed care, somatic practice, recovery principles, and integration.

My own healing led me here, but it does not define the limits of whom I can support. Years of sitting beside others have taught me that every story is different, and every person deserves care that honors the whole of who they are.

How I work

What shapes our time together

Root cause, not symptom management

We go to what is actually underneath. The patterns, the survival strategies, the unconscious wounds that have been running the show. That is where lasting change lives.

Nervous system first

Real transformation cannot be forced or rushed. We work at the pace your body can actually integrate. Safety is not a luxury in this work. It is the condition for it.

Trauma-informed, always

Consent, choice, and your own authority over your experience are foundational. Nothing is imposed. You set the pace. I hold the space.

Direct and deeply compassionate

I will not soften the work into something more comfortable than it needs to be. And I will hold you with genuine care while we do it. Both are true at once.
