The approach

Illuminate. Heal. Transform.

Illuminate. Most people spend their lives managing what is on the surface. The anxiety. The patterns. The relationships that keep breaking the same way. Underneath all of it is something that has rarely been truly seen. We go there, together.

Heal. Insight alone rarely changes a life. This work goes toward where the wound actually lives, in the body, the nervous system, the grief that was never allowed to move, the identity that formed around survival instead of truth. It creates conditions for meaningful change: not coping, not managing, but working with what lies beneath the symptom.

Transform. Like the caterpillar that dissolves before it becomes the butterfly, real transformation asks you to loosen what you have been in order to become more fully what you are. This work supports the slow release of old patterns, armor, and survival identity, and invites lasting transformation as the person who was always there, beneath the wounds, has room to emerge.

The work is deep. It asks for patience and honesty. And you are held with care every step of the way.

Meet Heather

Healing has been the great devotion of my life.

For 18 years I have walked a dedicated ceremonial and spiritual path. For 15 years I have practiced therapeutic massage and body-centered healing. Over the past five years, I have guided and supported people through psychedelic preparation, medicine experiences, and integration.

Today, I bring those foundations together with trauma-informed practice, recovery principles, somatic work, and graduate study in psychedelic therapy.

Is this for you?

You are ready for the real work.

You do not need prior healing experience, a particular diagnosis, or a spiritual background. You need one thing: the willingness to go within and meet what has been waiting to be met.

Carrying trauma that has never fully healed

Childhood and developmental trauma, CPTSD, sexual, physical, emotional, or psychological abuse, attachment wounding. The wounds that shaped you and the survival patterns that have quietly been running your life ever since.

Struggling with addiction or recovery

Substance and process addictions, love and sex addiction, codependency, and betrayal bonding. Support for working with what is underneath the pattern, alongside your recovery community, not instead of it.

Living with anxiety, depression, or suicidal thoughts

The low hum that will not turn off. The weight that will not lift. The moments when it all feels like too much. This work is not a substitute for clinical or emergency care, and can support you alongside it. See our Safety & Scope page.

Preparing for or integrating a psychedelic experience

Before or after a journey with plant medicine, ketamine, or other expanded states. Experienced preparation and integration support from someone who knows this terrain from the inside, within professional and legal scope.

Grieving, dissociated, or in a season of transition

Loss and grief, dissociation, shame and self-worth, shadow work, or a life transition and identity change that has left you unmoored. You do not need to name it precisely to begin.

Navigating relational trauma or spiritual emergence

Relational and attachment wounds, complex or spiritually significant experiences that have shaken your sense of self. Whatever your faith or spiritual orientation, or none at all, you are welcome here.

Like the butterfly, you do not need to force the transformation. You simply need to be ready to enter the cocoon. The rest unfolds from there.
