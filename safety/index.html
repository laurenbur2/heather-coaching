Safety & Scope

What this work is, and isn't.

Clear information about scope of practice, credentials, and where to find emergency or clinical support.

If you are in crisis

Illuminated Integration is not an emergency, crisis, medical, or psychiatric service. If you are in immediate danger or may harm yourself or someone else, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department.

In the United States, call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, available 24/7.

What Heather's services are

Services offered here are educational, supportive, integrative, somatic, and coaching-based, within Heather's current professional scope. They draw on trauma-informed practice, somatic and nervous-system-informed bodywork, recovery principles, contemplative and ceremonial experience, and psychedelic preparation and integration support.

What Heather's services are not

They do not replace diagnosis, medical care, psychiatric treatment, psychotherapy, or emergency services. Heather is not a psychotherapist, licensed therapist, psychedelic therapist, mental-health clinician, or medical provider, and does not diagnose or treat any medical or psychiatric condition.

Heather is becoming an integrative psychedelic therapist and is currently pursuing an MA in Psychedelic Therapy, in progress, through AWE Ecstatic Mysticism and Ubiquity University. She is not yet licensed to practice as a therapist.

Scope by area of work

Transformational support and coaching is distinct from psychotherapy and does not replace licensed mental-health treatment. It may complement therapy when appropriate.

Psychedelic preparation and integration includes education, preparation, harm-reduction support, meaning-making, somatic support, and integration. Heather does not prescribe, supply, dispense, or medically supervise any substance, and makes no medical claims about outcomes.

Ceremonial and expanded-state support includes legal, intentional ceremony for grief, transitions, prayer, and meaning-making, as well as nonmedical preparation and integration support around experiences you undertake elsewhere through legally operating containers. Availability and scope of ceremonial or expanded-state support depend on location, applicable law, safety screening, and professional appropriateness. Heather does not supply, dispense, or administer any illegal or controlled substance.

Somatic healing and therapeutic bodywork is trauma-informed massage and somatic support. It does not diagnose, treat, or cure trauma, and does not guarantee any specific nervous-system outcome.

A note on testimonials

Testimonials on this site are shared with the individual's permission and reflect their own experience. Individual experiences vary, and testimonials do not guarantee outcomes for anyone else.
